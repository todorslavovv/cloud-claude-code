\chapter{Solutions to the Exercises}\label{app:solutions}

This appendix contains a solution or solution sketch for every exercise in
the notes. Click on an exercise number to jump back to the exercise.

Try each exercise seriously before you look here. If your answer differs,
do not just read the solution: find the step where your reasoning went a
different way, and make sure you understand why. Many exercises can be
solved in more than one way, so a correct answer reached by a different
route is just as good.
